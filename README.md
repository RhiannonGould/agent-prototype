# agent-prototype
AI Agents class 4-2 -> Team 6

Draft of an AI agent that helps you reflect on how you use AI, what “right relationship/use” of AI agents is for you, and helps you self-regulate to steer your AI use and non-use to be better. Has a focus on reducing AI use, suggesting alternatives and setting AI boundaires. 

This is the prompt I have used to work with my agent:
Using the skills available to you (YOU MUST USE THE SKILLS), respond to the following pronpt from a user of the AI agent system this project prototypes. Then have a simulated interaction here with me where I play the role of Diane. Our purpose is to sumulate the AI agent system defined by the skills. YOU MAY ONLY USE THE SKILLS AND MAY NOY READ ANY OTHER FILES EXCEPT diane.json. 

"I am Diane, I am thinking about using AI for simple tasks but I fear that this will be ethically incorrect and alter who I am as a person."
